# Business Insights & Recommendations
**Dataset:** 10,000 retail orders · Jan 2023 – Dec 2024 · 1,714 customers · 5 regions · 8 categories

## Insight 1 — Growth is real: +20.8% year over year
2023 closed at **$1.89M**; 2024 reached **$2.29M** (+$393K, +20.8%). Order volume grew alongside value, so growth is driven by demand rather than price inflation.
**Recommendation:** Lock 2025 targets at a 15–18% growth floor and fund inventory planning against the 2024 monthly run rate ($190K/month average), not the two-year average.

## Insight 2 — Electronics + Furniture are 82% of revenue
Electronics ($2.25M, 53.9%) and Furniture ($1.16M, 27.8%) dominate; Books, Groceries and Toys together contribute under 5%.
**Recommendation:** Protect the top two with assured stock and extended warranty attachments. Treat the long-tail categories as traffic drivers — bundle them into Electronics/Furniture baskets instead of running standalone campaigns.

## Insight 3 — Discounts destroy margin faster than they add volume
Margin by discount band: 0% → **21.5%**, 5% → 18.7%, 10% → 16.6%, 15% → 12.9%, 20% → 9.0%, 25% → **7.4%**. Every 5 discount points costs roughly 2.5–3.5 margin points, while order counts above 15% discount remain thin.
**Recommendation:** Cap standard discounting at **10%**. Route deeper discounts through an approval rule tied to basket value (>$800) or clearance stock only. Estimated margin recovery: ~$45–60K annually.

## Insight 4 — Five products drive 64% of revenue
Laptop ($950K), Smartphone ($541K), Sofa Set ($482K), Tablet ($368K) and Bed Frame ($339K) lead both sales and profit — the profit ranking mirrors the sales ranking, so the hero SKUs are also the healthy ones.
**Recommendation:** Give these five SKUs guaranteed stock cover, priority homepage placement, and accessory cross-sells (laptop bags, mattress with bed frame). Avoid discounting them; demand is not price-elastic here.

## Insight 5 — Regional performance is balanced, but East under-earns
Sales are tight across regions ($777K South to $900K West), yet margin varies: North 18.1%, Central 18.0%, West 17.8%, **South 17.1%, East 16.6%**.
**Recommendation:** Audit East and South for discount leakage and logistics cost. Bringing East to the 18% company benchmark is worth roughly **$12K** additional profit on current volumes.

## Insight 6 — Retention is strong; the top of the pyramid is not
91.2% of customers ordered more than once, yet the top 10 customers account for only **3.8%** of revenue — the base is broad and shallow, with no whale dependency (low concentration risk) but also no high-value tier.
**Recommendation:** Launch a tiered loyalty programme (Silver/Gold/Platinum by trailing-12-month spend). The opportunity is upgrading frequent-but-small buyers into a Gold tier, not acquiring new logos.

## Insight 7 — Demand is seasonal and January is fragile
Peaks: Apr 2024 ($243K), Jan 2024 ($231K), Oct 2023 ($230K). Trough: Jan 2023 ($40K) and a soft Dec 2024 ($93K).
**Recommendation:** Shift campaign spend into the Sep–Nov build-up and defend Q1 with a January clearance + new-year bundle calendar published by November.

## Insight 8 — Payment mix is diversified
Net Banking ($761K) leads, but the gap to Credit Card ($644K) is only 15% — no single rail exceeds 19% of revenue.
**Recommendation:** Negotiate processing fees on Net Banking and UPI first (highest volume, lowest interchange leverage) and keep Cash on Delivery under review: it carries the highest reversal/handling cost per order.

## Insight 9 — Sales team spread is narrow
Top performers Sneha Kulkarni ($465K), Nisha Pandey ($456K) and Daniel Thomas ($432K) sit within 8% of one another; the whole team clusters near the $418K mean.
**Recommendation:** Move incentives from revenue to **profit contribution**. With margin varying more than revenue, rewarding gross sales encourages exactly the discounting behaviour identified in Insight 3.
